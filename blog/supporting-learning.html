Supporting Your Child's Learning Journey at Home

Feb 12, 2026 — 7 min read

Simple, consistent routines and playful learning opportunities help children build skills outside the classroom. Parents can reinforce curiosity by asking open-ended questions and celebrating effort.

Tips for Parents

  • Set short, regular learning sessions
  • Create low-pressure environments for exploration
  • Encourage reflection and sharing after play
