Support

We're here to help you with anything you need.

Contact Support

If you need help, have questions, or want to report an issue:

📧 support@sensorystudio.online

Support Hours:
8:00 AM – 9:30 PM (Daily)

Response Times

Our team typically responds within 30 minutes to 1 hour during active support hours (8:00 AM – 9:30 PM).

In rare cases of high demand, responses may take slightly longer, but all enquiries are handled as a priority.

What We Can Help With

  • Account issues or login problems
  • Bug reports and technical errors
  • Game-related questions
  • Safety concerns or reporting users
  • General enquiries

Email Strike Appeals

If your enquiry is about an email strike, contact:

📧 appeal@sensorystudio.online

Important: You must include your email address in the message. Our system verifies this before sending it to a support team member.

Before You Contact Us

  • Check our Documents for policies
  • Provide clear details about your issue
  • Include screenshots if needed
