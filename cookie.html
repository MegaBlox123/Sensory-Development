Cookie Policy

Last updated: 28 January 2026

Sensory Studio uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, personalise content, enable social media features, and gather analytics. This policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices regarding them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our services, and improve your experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for core site functionality, security, and system administration. These cannot be disabled.
  • Performance cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site by collecting anonymous data about page interactions and navigation patterns.
  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences and settings to personalise your experience across visits.
  • Marketing cookies: Used to track visitors and understand their behaviour to deliver targeted content and advertisements.

Specific cookies we use

Our service uses cookies to:

  • Keep you logged in if you create an account
  • Remember your language and display preferences
  • Track page views and user engagement with analytics
  • Detect fraud and improve security
  • Comply with legal obligations

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use, such as analytics platforms and social media partners. We don't control these cookies, but you can manage them through those services or your browser settings.

How to manage your cookie preferences

You have full control over cookies:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when a cookie is being set.
  • Cookie consent tools: We provide a consent banner when you first visit where you can select your preferences.
  • Disabling cookies: Be aware that disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.

Cookie retention

Cookies are retained for varying periods depending on their purpose. Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser, while persistent cookies may remain for weeks or months.

Contact us

If you have questions about our use of cookies or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us at hello@sensorystudio.com.
