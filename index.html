Information for Parents

Practical guidance for supporting your child's learning in Sensory Studio experiences.

Our games are designed to be educational, inclusive, and safe. We recommend parents:

  • Set appropriate playtime limits and supervise young children.
  • Encourage cooperative play and discuss in-game learning outcomes.
  • Review platform safety settings and privacy controls.

For more detailed advice, see our Documents or contact support at support@sensorystudio.online.
