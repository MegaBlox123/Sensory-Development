Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 January 2026

Sensory Studio ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and your rights in connection with it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in several ways:

  • Information you provide: When you register for an account, contact support, sign up for newsletters, or otherwise communicate with us, we may collect your name, email address, username, and any other information you choose to provide.
  • Usage data: We automatically collect data about your interactions with our website and games, including IP address, browser type, device identifiers, page views, session duration, and in-game actions.
  • Cookies and tracking technologies: We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tools to remember preferences, analyze trends, and serve targeted content. Please see the Cookie Policy for details.
  • Third‑party data: If you link or log in using a social media account or other third-party service, we may receive certain information from that service depending on your settings (e.g., public profile, friend list).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information for purposes such as:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving our products and services.
  • Personalizing your experience and delivering relevant content.
  • Communicating with you about updates, promotions, and support.
  • Monitoring and preventing fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our policies.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With service providers who perform functions on our behalf (e.g., hosting, analytics).
  • When required by law or to respond to legal process.
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or those of our users.
  • In connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Access, update, or delete your account information by logging into your account or contacting us.
  • Opt out of marketing communications by following the unsubscribe link in emails.
  • Set your browser to block or delete cookies, although some features may not function properly.
  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you or ask us to restrict processing.

For requests, please contact us at privacy@sensorystudio.example. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain information as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. International Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. By using our services, you consent to such transfers.

7. Children

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Your continued use after changes indicates acceptance.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns, contact us at privacy@sensorystudio.online
